GIVE HOPE. MAKE A DIFFERENCE.

Your gift can move us forward.

Give directly to a participating ALS charity, back a fundraising team, or support ALS Catalyst Foundation, which runs Dancing to Beat ALS. Every option names who receives your gift before you give.

Choose an amount and who receives it.

A participating charity

Your gift goes directly to the ALS charity you choose and counts toward its campaign. ALS Catalyst takes no share of gifts to charity campaigns.

A fundraising team

Back a family, community or company team. Your gift goes directly to the charity that team is fundraising for.

ALS Catalyst Foundation

Support the Host Team. Gifts fund ALS Catalyst’s operations, the event platform and future grants, and aren’t counted toward any charity’s campaign.

Charities receive gifts to their campaigns directly and own those donor relationships. ALS Catalyst receives your contact details only if you choose to share them. A platform processing fee is shown at checkout. Gala tickets, votes and event sponsorships fund the event and don’t count toward charity campaign totals.
