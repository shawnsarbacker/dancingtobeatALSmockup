DIVISION GUIDE

Mission first, then scale.

Each charity competes in one of four divisions. We ask what your campaign’s gifts mainly fund first, and only then look at your organization’s size. Answer three quick questions to see your proposed division.

QUESTION 1 OF 3

PROPOSED DIVISION

Discovery

Organizations or programs directly performing substantial ALS research: therapy development, clinical research, biomarkers and scientific discovery.

Care & Support

Organizations or programs directly delivering ALS clinical care, home and community support, navigation, mobility, assistive technology and caregiver services.

Reach

Other established ALS nonprofits, often focused on fundraising, grantmaking and advocacy, with more than $2 million in annual revenue in any of the last three fiscal years.

Grassroots

Other ALS nonprofits below the Reach threshold, including campaigns run by families, individuals and community groups for an approved nonprofit.

Funding someone else’s research or care doesn’t qualify on its own. For organizations that do several things, the main purpose of the campaign decides. Celebrity, dancer and company involvement never change a division. Organizers make the final call, and divisions are fixed once a charity is accepted.
