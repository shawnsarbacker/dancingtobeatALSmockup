Your recommended division

This is a proposed division, subject to organizer assignment at acceptance. Your beneficiary and dancer do not determine placement.

Sponsor first. Cause always.

Enterprise
Business-sponsored, funded, or fielded teams, including employee teams. Examples: corporate employee teams, dance studios, local businesses.
Discovery
Institutions conducting ALS research or clinical care. Examples: hospital ALS centers, academic ALS clinics, university labs.
Reach
National ALS organizations, media outlets, or individuals with a national public platform. Examples: national ALS nonprofits and advocacy groups.
Grassroots
Individuals, families, friends, community groups, and regional or local organizations. Examples: church or school groups, local ALS support groups.

Apply in order: Enterprise → Discovery → Reach → Grassroots. Stop at the first “yes”.
