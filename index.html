WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES

Team gifts

Gifts to a team are designated to support that team’s chosen ALS beneficiary and are granted under the Foundation’s grant policy. Any fees will be disclosed before launch.

Registration & tickets

Team Dance Card fees, gala tickets and tables pay for the event and the Foundation’s operating costs. They don’t count toward a team’s fundraising total.

Gifts to the Foundation

Gifts made directly to ALS Catalyst Foundation fund its grants to promising ALS work and the costs of running the Foundation.
