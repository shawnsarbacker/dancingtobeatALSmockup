WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES

Gifts to charity campaigns

Go directly to the participating ALS charity, including gifts made through a supporter or company team. ALS Catalyst takes no share of gifts to charity campaigns.

Tickets, votes & sponsorships

Fund the event itself: the venue, production and livestream. They don’t count toward any charity’s campaign total.

Gifts to the Foundation

Gifts to ALS Catalyst Foundation and its Host Team fund its operations and future grants to promising ALS work.
