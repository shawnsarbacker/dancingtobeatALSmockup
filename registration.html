Reserve your place on the dance floor.

Your Team Dance Card is your registration fee: a place in the competition, with gala seats included in the Team & Gala Package. Choose the card that fits your team below.

Upgrades include the table location and matching sponsor recognition. Card and upgrade payments do not count toward team fundraising, leaderboard position, or Draw entries.

Table availability and upgrade rules

Tables are allocated first come, first served by payment date from the same inventory as sponsor tables. The floor plan may flex the ring-two/ring-three split as tables sell. The Presenting table is sponsor-only.

Proposed upgrade deadline: 60 days before the event. After the deadline, unsold premium tables return to sponsor sales or may be released as individual $350 seats.

Demo: no tables are reserved. Shared inventory, payment-date allocation, and the event deadline are not connected.

Seat face values are planning references, not tax-deductible amounts. Any charitable portion requires the fiscal sponsor’s confirmation of eligibility and the fair market value of all benefits.

What’s included

    Before you purchase

    • Team-raised gifts are designated to support your beneficiary and granted under the Foundation’s grant policy; any fees will be disclosed before launch. The Dance Card fee helps cover ALS Catalyst Foundation’s event and operating costs.
    • The fee is nonrefundable; gala seats can be transferred.
    • The fee does not count toward your team’s fundraising total.
    • Finalists receive extra comp seats on top of the card: a +1, plus a caregiver seat for a dancer living with ALS.
    • Donors choose whether to share their contact details with your beneficiary.
