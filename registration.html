Tell us about your organization.

Participating charities are U.S. 501(c)(3) public charities, or hospital and university ALS programs that receive gifts through their charitable parent.

Hospital and university programs usually receive gifts through their parent nonprofit, designated for the ALS program.

Funding someone else’s research or care doesn’t count as performing or delivering it. How divisions work →

Who leads your campaign?

Your campaign lead recruits supporters and company partners and is our main contact.

Who will dance for you?

Each charity nominates one dancer. If your campaign becomes a finalist, your dancer partners with a professional and competes at the gala. You can name your dancer later.

Charities choose their own dancer; there are no random draws.

No dance experience required.

Set your campaign goal.

Goals are aspirational. Missing a goal never creates a shortfall bill; it only affects finalist eligibility.
